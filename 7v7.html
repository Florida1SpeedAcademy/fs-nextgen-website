Common questions

GOOD TO KNOW.

Are 2027 tryout dates posted yet?

Not yet. Dates, times and locations will be shared once confirmed. Families can email their interest now.

What is the 2027 registration fee?

The 2027 FS NextGen 7v7 registration fee is $200 per athlete and must be paid before the first practice. It covers practice facility fees, game uniforms, coaching, speed and agility training, practice equipment, conditioning, football IQ and team operations. Sending the interest email or registration request does not collect payment or guarantee a roster spot. After submitting the registration form, parents continue directly to secure Stripe checkout to pay.

Where is the program based?

Florida Speed Academy serves Seminole County and surrounding Central Florida communities. Specific football practice and competition locations will be announced.

Can beginners express interest?

Yes. Families may ask about the program regardless of an athlete's current level. Final team eligibility, divisions and selections will be announced by the program.
