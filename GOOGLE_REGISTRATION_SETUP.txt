FS NEXTGEN — SET UP PRIVATE GOOGLE ATHLETE REGISTRATION

1. In Google Drive, open Google Forms (New → Google Forms) or go to forms.google.com.
2. Title: FS NextGen Athletics | 2027 Athlete Registration Interest.
3. Add required questions: Parent/Guardian Name (short answer), Contact Email (email), Contact Phone (phone), Athlete Full Name (short answer), Athlete Age (number 5–14), Program (multiple choice: Indoor Track — $150; Outdoor Track — $275; Indoor + Outdoor — $375), Parent/guardian confirmation checkbox.
4. Add optional questions: School, prior sports experience, questions for the coaches.
5. Do not collect card numbers, SSNs, or medical documents.
6. Under Responses, choose Link to Sheets → Create a new spreadsheet. Limit access to authorized coaches/staff.
7. Publish/share the responder form, copy the responder URL (not editor URL). Test submitting dummy information, then check Sheets receives it.
8. Update the website's REGISTER ATHLETE buttons to use your published responder URL when ready. Until then, the website includes an email inquiry and a Google Docs template.
9. Do not publicly share a single editable Google Doc containing athlete information.

2027 Track fees: Indoor $150 / Outdoor $275 / Combined $375.
