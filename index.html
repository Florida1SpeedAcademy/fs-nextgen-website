Who can join the track program?+

The 2027 track program serves ages 5–14. The academy will confirm your athlete’s season age-group eligibility.

When and where are practices?+

The track program lists Monday–Wednesday for track and group development, and Thursday for field events and mechanics. We serve Seminole County and surrounding Central Florida communities. Exact practice locations, times, and season dates are not published here; contact the academy for your athlete’s schedule before requesting enrollment.

How do the deposit and balance work?+

The total is $275. Your nonrefundable $50 deposit is credited toward that total, leaving $225 due January 15, 2027. Contact the academy to confirm payment arrangements.

How do I request enrollment?+

Open the athlete registration page to prepare an email interest request or use the document option. You must send the email to deliver your request. A coach will confirm enrollment and payment details; this process does not submit a signed agreement or collect payment online.

Where can I find meets and athlete results?+

Visit the academy’s meet schedule and athlete profiles on the current website.
