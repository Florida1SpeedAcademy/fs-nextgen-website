GIVE BACK · CHANGE A YOUNG LIFE

SUPPORT OUR ATHLETES.

Your donation to Florida Speed Academy goes beyond sports. It helps us support children and families in need with educational resources, tutoring and counseling throughout our program. Contributions can also help athletes access physical therapy and recovery support through our team when appropriate and available. Every gift helps us develop the whole child—in the classroom, in competition and in life.

EDUCATION & TUTORING

Help young athletes strengthen their academics through school-related resources and tutoring support.

COUNSELING & WELLBEING

Support access to counseling and guidance that promotes confidence, resilience and personal development.

PHYSICAL THERAPY SUPPORT

Help athletes who need physical therapy treatment or recovery services access appropriate team-connected support, subject to availability.

DONATE SECURELY  ↗

Donations are processed securely through Stripe. Services depend on athlete needs and program resources. This donation is separate from team registration and apparel purchases.
